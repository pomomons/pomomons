## Format rules (all videos)

- **1080x1920, 9:16, 60fps, H.264 + AAC.** All three platforms accept this one
  file.
- **7–25 seconds.** Short videos get rewatched, and rewatches count most.
  Where possible the last frame leads back into the first, so the video loops.
- **Hook in the first second.** Big text plus movement right away, with no
  logo intro.
- **Safe zone.** Captions sit near the top, under the platform's tabs. The
  bottom ~20% and the right edge are covered by the app's buttons and the post
  description, so nothing has to be read there.
- **Game sounds only.** Every sound comes from the game itself. TikTok and
  Reels let you add a trending song inside the app; if you do, keep it at
  about 20% so the game sounds still come through.
- **Ending:** a short end card with the logo and POMOMONS.IO, except on
  videos built to loop.
- **Honesty rules** (same as keyword-strategy.md): no Pokémon names or terms,
  no health or productivity claims, and every number shown has to match the
  code (15 species, 30 forms, shiny 1 in 500, dark 1 in 100).

## The list (38 ideas, about 5 weeks at one a day)

**A. The core loop (hooks for new viewers)**
1. **"I turned my Pomodoro timer into a monster-catching game."** The full
   loop: timer, encounter, catch, blend, evolve, end card. ~20s.
2. **"POV: you finally finish your study session."** The last 5 seconds of
   the timer tick down, then a wild mon pops out and gets caught. ~10s, loops.
3. **"What happens when the timer hits 0:00."** A slow countdown, a hard cut
   to the encounter, the catch. ~9s.
4. **"Studying for 25 minutes gets you this."** Time-lapse of the timer
   running, then a quick run of 4 different catches (one per session). ~14s.
5. **"How it works in 3 steps."** Focus → catch → evolve, one caption per
   step. ~15s.

**B. Meet the Mons (a weekly series, one species per video)**
6–13. **"Meet TOMOTOT."** The wild encounter, its name, type and level, then
   its evolution. Run it for Tomotot, Chilino, Avocuddle, Mushkin, Pinapip,
   Bluble, Spud and Pumplet. The three-stage lines (Tomotot, Chilino,
   Avocuddle) show both evolutions. ~12s each.

**C. Rarity**
14. **"What a 1-in-500 shiny looks like."** A shiny encounter with sparkles,
    then the catch. ~10s.
15. **"The 1-in-100 dark variant."** The same format with a dark mon. ~10s.
16. **"Normal vs dark vs shiny."** The same mon three times, side by side.
    ~8s.
17. **"The rarest mon in the game."** Donot, which shows up 10x less often
    than the others. ~10s.

**D. Evolution**
18. **"Feeding my mon a smoothie until it evolves."** Blend, feed, evolve.
    ~15s.
19. **"Every evolution in 20 seconds."** The reveal moment of all 15
    evolutions, back to back. ~23s.
20–22. **"Guess what it evolves into."** A blacked-out silhouette, a 3-second
    countdown ("comment your guess"), then the evolution plays. Use
    Cocokid, Pitapal and Manglet. ~12s each.
23. **"Would you press STOP?"** The evolution starts, the cursor hovers over

25. **"Blending extra mons into smoothies."** Blend 3 in a row, with the
    blender sound. ~12s.

**F. Collection**
27. **"Every PomoMon in 15 seconds."** All 30 forms, one after another. ~15s.

**G. Questions for the comments**

31. **"Which flavour are you?"** Sweet / Spicy / Savory / Sour / Bitter,

32. **"Rate these mons 1–10."** Five mons, one at a time. ~12s.

**H. Features**

34. **"Your companion levels up while you study."** The XP bar fills and LV

35. **"Set it to however long you focus."** Click the timer from 20 up to 90

36. **"The Pomodoro technique in 15 seconds."** Focus, short break, and a long

**I. Your story (text on screen; you can add your own voice later)**
