Frequently asked questions

How the timer works, how catching works, and what happens to your collection. If something here is still unclear, ask in the Discord.

What is PomoMons?

PomoMons is a free Pomodoro timer with a creature-collecting game attached. You set a focus session and work until it ends. When it does, a wild food-themed pixel monster called a Pomomon appears, and you throw a tomato to catch it. Caught Pomomons go into your Pomodex, level up, and evolve. It runs in any modern browser with no download and no account.

Is PomoMons free?

Yes, completely. There is no paid tier, no ads, nothing locked behind a sign-up, and no credit card anywhere. The only optional extra is giving an email address to receive a backup code for your collection, and the app never requires it.

How long is a Pomodoro?

The classic Pomodoro is 25 minutes of focus followed by a 5 minute break, with a longer 15 minute break after every fourth focus session. PomoMons starts you slightly longer than the classic, at 30 minutes of focus with the same 5 and 15 minute breaks. All three are adjustable with the arrows above the clock: focus from 20 to 90 minutes, breaks from 1 to 90. There is more on where those numbers come from in the guide to the Pomodoro Technique.

Do I need an account to use PomoMons?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the page and press START. Your level, XP and collection are saved in your own browser, so nothing is tied to an account.

Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes. The timer is measured against the clock rather than counted frame by frame, so a session that should finish at a certain time finishes then even if the tab was in the background or the browser throttled it. You can also tick “Notify when timer ends” in the settings menu to get a notification when a session is up.

How do I catch a Pomomon?

Finish a focus session. A wild Pomomon appears on the encounter screen, and you press CATCH to throw a tomato at it. The catch always succeeds, so a Pomomon you meet is a Pomomon you keep. You can also press RUN AWAY to skip the encounter and go straight to your break.

How many Pomomons are there?

There are 30 Pomomon forms across 16 base species, numbered #1 to #30 in the Pomodex. Fourteen of the 30 are evolutions, which a caught Pomomon reaches at level 16, 20 or 36 depending on the species. Evolved forms also appear in the wild once your own level is within two of the level they evolve at, so you can catch one outright rather than raising it, and base forms keep appearing at every level. Every Pomomon belongs to one of five flavour types: Savory, Sweet, Spicy, Sour or Bitter. The full Pomodex lists all of them.

What are shiny and dark Pomomons?

They are the two rare variants. A shiny Pomomon is gold-tinted with sparkles and appears in roughly 0.2% of encounters, or about 1 in 500. A dark Pomomon is near-black and appears in about 1% of encounters, or 1 in 100. The remaining 98.8% are normal. Shiny is rolled first and wins outright, so a Pomomon is never both at once.

How do I move my collection to another device?

Open the settings menu and choose Save. You can copy your save code straight from the screen, or have it emailed to you. On the other device, open the same menu, choose Restore, and paste the code in. Restoring replaces whatever collection is on that device, so it is a move rather than a merge.

Can I use PomoMons offline, or install it?

Both. PomoMons is an installable web app, so your browser can add it to your home screen or desktop and open it in its own window with no address bar. Once it has loaded one time, the timer, the encounters and your collection all work with no connection at all.

Will I lose my collection if I clear my browser data?

Yes. Your collection lives in your browser's own storage on that one device, so clearing site data, using a private window, or switching browser loses it. This is exactly what the save code is for: get one from the settings menu and your collection can be restored anywhere.

Does PomoMons track me?

There are no tracking cookies, no advertising scripts and no third-party profiles. The site counts anonymous page views with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data. Your sessions, level and collection never leave your device unless you ask for a save code by email.
